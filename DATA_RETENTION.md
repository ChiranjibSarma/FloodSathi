# Data retention

Retention is configuration-driven. Initial recommended review points—not legal conclusions—are: audio 30 days, images 90 days, closed operational requests 365 days, medical fields 90 days after closure, volunteer identity records for the verification term plus 180 days, and audit records 7 years. Validate with Assam/India counsel and humanitarian governance. A scheduled worker should delete private objects first, anonymise eligible rows, record an audit event, and skip legal holds.
